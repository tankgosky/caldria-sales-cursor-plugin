# Landed Cost Proposal

1. Use only evidenced sourcing candidates and current quote-request quantities.
2. Separately calculate product, international freight, duty/tax, domestic freight, payment and risk buffer, total landed cost, target margin, and suggested delivered price.
3. Record every assumption, source, currency, and confidence limitation.
4. Save the result as `pending_review` with `save_pricing_proposal`.
5. This is internal analysis. Never modify final quote fields or communicate pricing, delivery, or payment commitments externally.
