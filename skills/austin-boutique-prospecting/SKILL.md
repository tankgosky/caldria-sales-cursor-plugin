# Austin Boutique Prospecting

1. Research independent Austin boutiques only through public business websites, directories, maps, and business social profiles.
2. Save the business name, Austin/TX location, website, public business email or contact page, category, fit score, rationale, and every source URL.
3. Deduplicate by website and public email before saving.
4. Never infer personal email addresses, use purchased lists, submit contact forms, or contact a suppressed lead.
5. Stop at 15 net-new leads per business day and report missing evidence instead of guessing.
